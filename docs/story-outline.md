# Story Outline
(many spoilers ahead if you care for such a thing lol. Otherwise, here is the critical plot
outline for project )
## Premise 

The heros are scattered, living lives of confused
rebellion with the manner in which things function. There
are grand empires and rebel factions, but the hero party 
are strangers to every community it would seem

## Main Conflict

After gaining ground against the mad array of forces
against them. The party then realizes there is an 
even grander existential conflict. Dr.Deliver_End
, his Network of End is a cybernetic armada of units
that had operated in parallel to the plot, to end
all this is earth

Deliver_End had been banished from academia his entire
life, when a being finds him. And trains him
in the most advanced degrees of cybernetic engineering.
Certifying him as a "doctor" , 

## Major Revalations

1. The apparent central nodes are tactical interfaces, not
whole beings. All the won battles were just setbacks
to Deliver_End
2. The Network of End is not a simple enemy faction, but
a complex cyber network
3. Then the plot twists entirely
4. A group of beings born in the void of the universe,
"holtzmen brains" but with the powers of gods.
5. The Boltzmen Gods engineered 
the entire plot of Dr.Deliver_end
6. The party must align with Dr.Deliver_End to defeat the 
Boltzmen Gods. They endeavor to a Tout te monde
7. Nearly all characters perish in the final sequence
8. A few escape, their fate is left ambiguous
9. Dr.Deliver_End's last sight was in a explosion with
the Boltzmen gods at every flank
10. An echo at the end over a shadowed earth implies 
Deliver_End may still exist

## Network of End

Each named node is a local locus: physically
encounterable interface of a larger distributed
intelligence and military array. Defeating a
locus causes a strategic setback, but fails to truly
destroy the entity

1. Artilleran - loci - walking bipedal mechanical body with
   canons. Has a larger existence as a giant artillery
   installation. Possibly on the moon
2. Meta-4  loci - floating tactical device. Larger system
airship summon
3. Helixion - loci - small little heli - 4 layered 
network. Outer helicopters, forward base, critical module,
then small heli
4. Aquaran - loci - single giant carrier, true
summon Giant sea armada
5. Lightryr - loci - small grenade-like cannister.
true summon - giant lightning summon that travels alongside
the airships most times
6. Whrythr - loci - modest speedy tank - true summon -
giant roaming distributed car fleet
7. Dr.Deliver_End - loci changes - final summon amibguous
cyber cybe
8. Iron-Ie - loci - barricade/cover system - final summon
giant fortress
9.  Radio - loci - a speaker that shoots odd frequency
energy rather than sound alone. Distributed system will
be a giant stage
10. Engine - loci - a bipedal simple engineer with interesting
intricate designs. Distributed system summon - a giant 
literal engine hidden and often moved by the roving Whrythr
fleet
